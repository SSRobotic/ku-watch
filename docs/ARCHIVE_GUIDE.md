# Original-language archive

Original Thai notes remain at their source paths so maintenance tools and their links can still be traced. They may describe older firmware or unfinished work at the time of writing. The main English README and `docs/VALIDATION.md` take precedence for the final handoff status.

`archive/acceptance-thai/` preserves the final Word checklist and its original supporting comparison. `presentation/` is a dated 15 October 2026 slide snapshot. The larger academic/development decks are private release attachments. Their language and historical statements have not been silently rewritten.

The repository is an initial source import. It does not claim to contain every build cache, duplicate ZIP, raw route or credential-bearing firmware backup from the workstation.

The root `DELIVERY_SHA256.json` is the imported source-archive checksum record. `docs/REPOSITORY_MANIFEST.json` is the checksum inventory of this GitHub handoff. They intentionally describe different payloads.
